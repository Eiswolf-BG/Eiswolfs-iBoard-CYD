EISWOLFS IBOARD

Install Utility
Version: v1.0

A small desktop dashboard: your Mac's live stats, shown on a Cheap Yellow Display next to your desk.

1. CYD firmware
1. Connect your CYD via USB cable
2. Insert a FAT32-formatted microSD card
3. Click "Connect" below and select the port
4. Wait for the flash to finish, then set up WiFi (2.4 GHz only) on the device
Requires Chrome, Edge, or Opera (Web Serial API)
2. Mac app (Apple Silicon only)
Reads your Mac's CPU/GPU/RAM/temperature/fan stats and sends them to the CYD. First launch: right-click the app → "Open" (the app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized).
