PyInstaller - GPLv2-or-later WITH a bootloader exception

Eiswolfs iBoard's Mac app is packaged into a standalone .app bundle
using PyInstaller (https://pyinstaller.org). PyInstaller itself is
licensed under the GNU General Public License v2 or later, but it
carries an explicit, additional exception for its "bootloader" (the
small stub that starts a frozen app) that is embedded into every app
PyInstaller builds:

    "The bootloader [...] may be distributed under the terms of your
    choosing, provided that you distribute it only in combination with
    PyInstaller. [...] This exception does not however invalidate any
    other reasons why the resulting executable file might be covered by
    the GNU General Public License."

In practice: this exception exists specifically so that PyInstaller can
be used to build and distribute closed-source and other non-GPL
software (including commercial ones) without that software itself
being placed under the GPL. See:

https://github.com/pyinstaller/pyinstaller/blob/develop/COPYING.txt

for PyInstaller's own full license text and the exact wording of the
exception.
